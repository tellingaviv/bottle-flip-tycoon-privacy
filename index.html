1. Who we are

Contact: clementinisimone@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • Your game progress is saved on your device. We do not receive it.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and the Game has no account system.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects device and advertising identifiers. This is the only routine collection of personal data connected with the Game.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • You cannot upload photos or files. Avatars are a fixed set drawn into the Game.

3. Data we collect, and why

3.1 Data stored only on your device

Your progress — money, bottles, upgrades, prestige, unlocked cosmetics, settings, language and statistics — is written to the Game’s local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us and we have no way to read it. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If your device or store account backs up app data (for example iCloud or Android Backup), that copy is governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy, not this one.

3.2 Data collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded ads (which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward) and may show banner ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve, cap, measure and protect those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (usually city-level) location can be derived;
  • ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, clicked or completed;
  • signals used to detect invalid traffic and ad fraud.

Google acts as an independent controller for this processing. What Google does with the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3.3 Crash and performance data

Apple and Google may share aggregated crash reports and performance statistics with us through App Store Connect and the Google Play Console. These are provided in aggregate form and are not used to identify individual players.

3.4 What we never collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No precise GPS location.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or file access.
  • No special-category (“sensitive”) data as defined in Article 9 GDPR — no health, biometric, religious, political or similar data.
  • No user-uploaded content of any kind.

4. Consent for personalised advertising

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your choice about personalised advertising before serving ads, using Google’s consent management interface (UMP). You may accept personalised ads, or refuse them and still play — refusing means you will see non-personalised ads instead, which are chosen from general context rather than from a profile.

On iOS, a separate system prompt (App Tracking Transparency) asks whether apps may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not made available.

You can change your advertising choice at any time from the Game’s settings, and you can reset or delete your advertising identifier from your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

10. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country, which in Italy is 14). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Where the Game is distributed to a mixed or child audience, or where a user is identified as a child by the store’s own age signals, advertising is restricted to non-personalised, child-appropriate ads in line with Google Play’s Families policy and the App Store’s Kids-related rules.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data, please write to clementinisimone@gmail.com and we will act promptly.

11. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data concerning you, and obtain a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing, including profiling for advertising purposes;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

An honest caveat about how to exercise them: because we hold no account and no identifier of yours, we generally cannot find “your” data — there is nothing on our side to look up. Requests concerning advertising data should be addressed to Google, which holds it; requests concerning purchases should be addressed to the store that processed them. We will help you route a request if you write to us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

12. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Game removes the local save from your device. To remove advertising data, reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings and, if you wish, submit a request to Google. If your store account backs up app data to the cloud, delete that backup from your device or account settings as well.

13. Security

Because game data stays on your device, its security depends chiefly on your device: keep it updated and protected by a passcode or biometric lock. Any data handled by Google or Apple is protected under their own security arrangements.

14. Features that may be added later

The Game currently works entirely offline apart from advertising. Online features are planned — global leaderboards, optional player accounts, cloud saves, friends lists and timed events. They are not active today. If and when they are introduced:

  • they will require a server, and additional data (such as a nickname you choose, a pseudonymous account identifier and your submitted score) will be transmitted and stored;
  • this policy will be updated before those features are switched on, with a new “last updated” date and a clear description of what is collected;
  • where an online feature is optional, it will be opt-in, and the Game will remain fully playable without it;
  • any nickname is chosen by you and shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please do not use your real name or any personal information in it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes or the law does. The current version is always the one published at this address, and the date at the top tells you when it last changed. Substantial changes will also be announced in the Game.
